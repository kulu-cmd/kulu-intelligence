# "The Long Walk Home" — 2min Cinematic Film Treatment

## Overview

A 2-minute cinematic documentary/intro video for Kulu Intelligence, following the 7-frame narrative structure from the About page. The film tells Kamil's personal story — from South Africa, through London and Singapore, back home — and why Kulu Intelligence exists.

---

## Tone

Warm, personal, slightly melancholic turning hopeful. Think: Apple "Shot on iPhone" meets a South African tourism film. No corporate B-roll. No stock footage. Real places, real light.

## Voiceover

Kamil narrates in first person, conversational — like telling the story over a beer. Record it raw on a voice memo first to get the cadence natural, then re-record clean. Don't script it word-for-word — bullet points and talk.

## Music

One continuous track. Start sparse (solo piano or soft guitar), build tension in the middle (synth pads layer in), resolve warm at the end. Something like Olafur Arnalds or Nils Frahm — ambient, textural, human. License from Artlist or Musicbed.

## Shot List

### Frame 1 — "Born in South Africa" (0:00–0:15)

- Open on black. 2 seconds of silence.
- Wide aerial drone shot at golden hour — Johannesburg skyline, slow push-in. Hold for 4 seconds.
- Cut to: close-up of hands on a keyboard, warm side-light. Rack focus to a window showing SA landscape.
- **VO:** *"I was born in South Africa."*
- Title card fades in: **Born in South Africa.** (white on black, Bricolage Grotesque)

### Frame 2 — "Grew up somewhere else" (0:15–0:35)

- Quick cuts, 1.5s each: London rain on a window. Singapore skyline at night, neon reflections. A crowded tube platform. Hawker centre steam.
- Shoot handheld, slightly underexposed — these places are beautiful but they're not *home*. Cool colour grade (teal/grey).
- **VO:** *"I grew up abroad. London, Singapore. Different worlds. Same feeling that something was missing."*
- Between cuts: a 0.5s flash of black with the city name in small eyebrow text.

### Frame 3 — "Built machines" (0:35–0:55)

- Tight shot: multiple monitors with trading terminals, green/red numbers scrolling. Bloomberg-style.
- Slow dolly along a row of empty office desks. Fluorescent light. Sterile.
- Close-up: Kamil's face reflected in a dark monitor. Expressionless.
- **VO:** *"I built trading systems for investment banks. High frequency, high stakes. Impressive on paper."*
- The colour grade here is cold — desaturated, blue-ish.

### Frame 4 — "But that's no fun" (0:55–1:05)

- **THE PIVOT.** Hard cut to warmth.
- Wide shot: a stoep (porch) somewhere in SA. Golden light. A dog lying in the sun. Someone laughs off-camera.
- **VO:** *"But that's no fun."* (said with a smile in the voice)
- Hold the shot for 3 full seconds. Let it breathe. The music shifts here — the piano resolves, warmth enters.
- Optional: title card "But that's no fun." on a coral background, 1.5 seconds.

### Frame 5 — "Home is where the people are" (1:05–1:25)

- Montage of real SA moments. Shoot these yourself:
  - Kamil at a braai, talking to someone, gesturing
  - A family member on their phone, Kamil leaning over showing them something
  - Laughter. Real laughter, not staged.
  - Someone's face lighting up — the "aha moment" when AI clicks
- Warm colour grade. Golden hour everything. Shallow depth of field.
- **VO:** *"Back home, I started helping the people around me. My family. My friends. Small things — automating a form, sorting their inbox, finding leads they didn't know existed."*

### Frame 6 — "Small automations" (1:25–1:40)

- Screen recordings mixed with real footage:
  - A quick screen capture of an automation running (a spreadsheet populating, a chatbot responding)
  - Cut to: someone's relieved face. A laugh. A "no way" reaction.
  - Hands shaking. A WhatsApp message: "bro this saved me 3 hours"
- **VO:** *"Small automations. Countless hours saved. Not for banks — for real people."*
- The music is building now. Hopeful.

### Frame 7 — "That's why Kulu Intelligence exists" (1:40–2:00)

- Wide shot: Kamil walking into a boardroom/office, greeting people. Warm.
- Cut to: the Kulu. wordmark on a screen/wall/card — hero moment for the brand.
- Final shot: Kamil looking into camera. A slight nod. Maybe a half-smile.
- **VO:** *"That's why I started Kulu Intelligence. AI, made human."*
- Fade to the wordmark on indigo. Coral period springs in (match the website animation). Hold 3 seconds.
- End card: kulu.co.za · hello@kulu.co.za

## Production Notes

### What you need

- A camera operator with a cinema lens (or a friend with a Sony A7 + 35mm f/1.4)
- A drone for the opening shot (or buy a stock aerial of JHB — the one exception)
- Golden hour — shoot everything between 4:30–6pm
- Screen recordings of real automations you've built
- 2–3 willing friends/family for the "helping people" montage

### What you don't need

- A studio. Shoot in real spaces — homes, offices, stoeps.
- Actors. Real reactions are 10x better.
- A massive budget. This is a one-camera, two-day shoot.

### Editing rhythm

- Frames 1–3: cuts get faster (building tension)
- Frame 4: hard stop, long hold (the breath)
- Frames 5–7: steady, warm, resolving

### Colour grade

- Frames 1–3: cool, slightly desaturated (abroad, corporate)
- Frame 4: the shift — warmth floods in
- Frames 5–7: warm, golden, SA sunlight, slight film grain
